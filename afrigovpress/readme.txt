The afrigov design system for WordPress: accessible pages for ministries, agencies and public services in Africa.

== Description ==

afrigovPress puts the afrigov design system on a WordPress site. Choose your country and the theme sets the colours, the flag and the words of the official website banner. Write pages and news in the editor as usual; the theme lays them out with afrigov's components and checks nothing more is needed for them to be accessible.

* An official website banner with "How you know this is official".
* Country packs: Ghana, Kenya, Morocco, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, or a neutral look.
* A header for a ministry or an agency, with the crest as the logo and the parent organisation under the name.
* News as a dated list with thumbnails, and articles with a lead image.
* A footer with link columns, the postal address and social links.
* A title, description, sharing tags and structured data written for every page from what the editor already wrote. If an SEO plugin is active the theme leaves this to the plugin.
* Works with the block editor and the classic editor.

== Setup ==

1. Appearance, Customise, Site Identity: the organisation's name and its crest as the logo.
2. Appearance, Customise, Government and country: the country, the line under the name, the address and social accounts.
3. Appearance, Menus: the main navigation, two footer columns, and the footer bar.
4. Settings, Reading: a static home page and a News page for posts.
